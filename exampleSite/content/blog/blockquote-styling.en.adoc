Quotations should never fade into surrounding body copy.
When reading an article, a reader needs an immediate visual cue that marks an excerpted voice, an interview response, poetry, or historical documentation.
Toph provides an editorial blockquote design system crafted for both AsciiDoc and Markdown.

Every quotation features an accent rule on the inline start, a subtle tinted background, refined asymmetric corner rounding, and relaxed italic typography.


== AsciiDoc quote blocks

AsciiDoc provides first-class syntax for quote blocks using the `[quote]` attribute and four underscores (`____`).
This syntax natively supports block titles, author attribution, and cited publication sources.

.Universal Declaration of Human Rights
[quote, United Nations General Assembly, Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948)]
____
All human beings are born free and equal in dignity and rights.
They are endowed with reason and conscience and should act towards one another in a spirit of brotherhood.
____

AsciiDoc quote blocks also support multiple paragraphs and embedded lists:

.Principles of Digital Autonomy
[quote, Declaration of Digital Autonomy]
____
We call for the adoption of principles that place technology in service of humanity:

. In service of the people who use it
. Informed consent and transparency
. Empowering individual and collective digital action
. Protecting privacy and fundamental rights by design
____


== Verse blocks for poetry and lyrics

Standard HTML `<pre>` tags default to monospace Courier type, which strips song lyrics and poetry of their literary feel.
AsciiDoc solves this with the `[verse]` block.
Toph styles verse blocks with the same visual container as quotes while preserving line breaks and rendering in the clean site body typeface.

.Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening
[verse, Robert Frost, New Hampshire (1923)]
____
The woods are lovely, dark and deep,
But I have promises to keep,
And miles to go before I sleep,
And miles to go before I sleep.
____
